IRCTC Refund Rules (Updated April 2026)

---------------------------------------
CONFIRMED TICKETS (CNF)
---------------------------------------

1. More than 72 hours before departure:
- Maximum refund.
- Only minimum flat cancellation charges are deducted.

2. Between 72 hours and 24 hours:
- 25% of total fare is deducted.

3. Between 24 hours and 8 hours:
- 50% of total fare is deducted.

4. Less than 8 hours before departure:
- No refund (0% refund).

---------------------------------------
WAITLISTED (WL) TICKETS
---------------------------------------

1. If ticket remains waitlisted after chart preparation:
- Ticket is automatically cancelled.
- Full refund is issued.

2. If cancelled manually before chart:
- Small cancellation charge (~₹20 + GST) may apply.

---------------------------------------
RAC (RESERVATION AGAINST CANCELLATION)
---------------------------------------

- Partial refund is issued after deduction of charges.

---------------------------------------
TATKAL TICKETS
---------------------------------------

1. Confirmed Tatkal ticket:
- No refund on cancellation.

2. Waitlisted Tatkal ticket:
- Partial refund allowed.

---------------------------------------
CHART PREPARATION RULE
---------------------------------------

- E-tickets cannot be cancelled online after chart preparation.
- User must file TDR (Ticket Deposit Receipt) for refund.

---------------------------------------
TRAIN CANCELLATION
---------------------------------------

- If train is cancelled by Railways:
- Full refund is issued automatically.

---------------------------------------
TRAIN DELAY (>3 HOURS)
---------------------------------------

- If train is delayed more than 3 hours and passenger does not travel:
- Full refund is allowed after filing TDR before departure.

---------------------------------------
PARTIALLY CONFIRMED TICKETS
---------------------------------------

- Mixed CNF + WL tickets:
- Refund rules depend on cancellation timing and TDR filing.

---------------------------------------
TDR (TICKET DEPOSIT RECEIPT)
---------------------------------------

- Required in cases like:
  - Late train
  - Train diverted
  - Passenger not travelled
  - AC failure
  - Chart prepared cases

- Time limits:
  - Usually within 72 hours of departure or arrival.

---------------------------------------
OTHER IMPORTANT RULES
---------------------------------------

- Boarding station can be changed up to 30 minutes before departure.
- OTP verification required for agent-based ticket cancellations.
- No refund if:
  - Ticket not cancelled before allowed time
  - TDR not filed when required

---------------------------------------
SYSTEM NOTES FOR AI
---------------------------------------

- Refund depends on:
  1. Ticket status (CNF / RAC / WL)
  2. Time of cancellation
  3. Train conditions (delay/cancelled)
  4. Tatkal or normal booking

- Always explain refund using above factors.
